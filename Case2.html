Air Buddy

AR wayfinding for airports that also helps you keep track of the people you travel with.

Timeline
2023
Type

Role

Long story short

AR wayfinding for busy airports, with a people finder built in.

Air Buddy is a concept app that guides travellers to their gate with AR. It also works as a people finder, so families can locate children or elderly relatives, which makes the trip easier and safer.
