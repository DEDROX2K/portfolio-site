Clear Breath

A behavioural therapy app for quitting cigarettes, built around a faster way to log.

Timeline
2024
Type
Concept app
Role
UX & product design

Long story short

I cut logging a cigarette from 7 steps to 3.

Clear Breath is a concept CBT app that helps people quit smoking. Logging is the habit the whole app depends on, so I made it about 3× quicker than competing apps by reducing the flow from seven steps to three.
